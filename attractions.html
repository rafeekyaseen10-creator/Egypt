My favorit places to see in Egypt

The Pyramids

The Great Pyramid of Giza is one of the most famous ancient monuments in the world. It was built in Egypt more than 4,500 years ago as a tomb for Pharaoh Khufu. The pyramid was originally about 146 meters tall and was the tallest man-made structure in the world for thousands of years. It is made from millions of large stone blocks and is part of the Giza pyramid complex. The Great Pyramid is one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and is the only one that still largely survives today.

The Grand Egyotian museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) is one of Egypt’s most important cultural landmarks, located near the Giza Pyramids. It houses a vast collection of ancient Egyptian artifacts, including many treasures from the time of the pharaohs. The museum combines modern architecture with Egypt’s rich history and provides visitors with an opportunity to learn about ancient Egyptian civilization. It is also home to the complete collection of artifacts from the tomb of King Tutankhamun, making it a remarkable destination for history and archaeology enthusiasts.
