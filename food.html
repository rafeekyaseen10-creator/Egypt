My favorit Food in Egypt

kushari

Kushari is a popular Egyptian dish made from rice, lentils, macaroni, and chickpeas, topped with crispy fried onions and a spicy tomato sauce. It is a filling and affordable meal that combines different flavors and textures. Kushari is commonly served in restaurants and street-food shops throughout Egypt and is enjoyed by many people as a traditional comfort food.
